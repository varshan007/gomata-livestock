# GoMata: Platform Capabilities Guide (End-User Perspective)

Welcome to the GoMata Livestock Monitoring Platform! This guide outlines exactly what you, as a farm manager, veterinarian, or staff member, can currently do with the application.

Everything in this platform is designed to run automatically in the background, surfacing critical information to you exactly when you need it.

---

## 1. Real-Time Dashboard & Monitoring
When you log into the GoMata interface, you get a live, up-to-the-second view of your entire herd. 

* **Live Vitals:** The dashboard continuously updates with each animal's temperature, heart rate, and battery levels without requiring you to refresh the page.
* **Bird's-Eye View:** Instantly see how many animals are healthy, how many have warnings, and how many are in critical condition.
* **Activity Tracking:** At a glance, understand the general activity of your herd (e.g., how many animals are grazing, resting, or moving).

## 2. AI-Powered Health Diagnostics
You no longer need to manually stare at charts or guess what a heart rate spike means. The platform acts as a 24/7 digital veterinarian.

* **Automated Diagnosis:** If an animal's temperature or heart rate fluctuates, the AI instantly analyzes the recent history and determines if it's a minor fluctuation, a developing fever, or a critical emergency like heat stress or infection.
* **Actionable Advice:** Instead of just telling you "Cow 001 is hot," the platform gives you a plain-English explanation of exactly what might be wrong and prescribes immediate action steps for your staff to take.
* **Smart Filtering:** The system automatically deduplicates identical alerts for 10 minutes so your phone and dashboard don't get spammed if a cow's temperature stays high.

## 3. Instant Emergency SMS Alerts
You don't need to be staring at the GoMata dashboard to know when a crisis happens.

* **Text Message Notifications:** The moment the AI confirms a Warning or Critical health emergency, the platform instantly sends a customized text message to the mobile phones of your registered Veterinarians and Farm Administrators.

## 4. GPS Tracking & Geofencing
Keep your animals safe and secure with intelligent boundary tracking.

* **Virtual Fences:** You can define specific "Safe Zones" on the map (like a pasture or a barn).
* **Escape Detection:** As the hardware collars send GPS coordinates, the platform constantly checks if the animal is inside its assigned zone.
* **Breach Alerts:** If a cow wanders outside the virtual fence, an emergency "Geofence Breach" alert is triggered instantly, notifying your team to intercept the animal.

## 5. Automated Staff Accountability & Escalation
When an emergency happens, the platform ensures that *someone* handles it before it's too late.

* **Instant Delegation:** When a High or Critical alert fires (like a fence breach or a fever), the system automatically looks up who is currently on shift and assigns the task directly to a specific Farm Hand or Vet.
* **Dashboard Popups:** A red alert popup will appear on the assigned user's screen with the animal's details and an **[ Acknowledge ]** button.
* **Managerial Escalations:** The platform starts a silent countdown timer (e.g., 10 minutes) the moment an emergency is detected. If the assigned staff member fails to click "Acknowledge" within that timeframe, the platform automatically escalates the emergency and re-assigns it to a senior Admin or Manager to ensure no animal is left ignored.

---

### Summary of Your Workflow
As a user of GoMata today:
1. You outfit your cattle with the hardware collars.
2. You draw your safe zones on the map.
3. You go about your day.
4. **If anything goes wrong:** You receive a text message, a dashboard popup, a plain-English AI diagnosis of the issue, and a designated staff member is immediately held accountable to fix it!
